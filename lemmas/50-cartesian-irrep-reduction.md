# Lemma 50: Cartesian Irrep Reduction

## Scope

- Status: Note. This lemma works in the three-dimensional spatial tensor language appropriate to the MVP free-fall sector.
- Status: Proven. It is class-limited to local parity-even nonspinning primitive families.

## Imported Representation Fact

- Status: Imported from prior work. In three spatial dimensions, every Cartesian tensor decomposes under `O(3)` into a direct sum of:
  trace descendants built with `\delta_{ij}`,
  ordinary STF tensors,
  and epsilon-dual pseudo tensors.
- Status: Imported from prior work. Mixed-symmetry Young sectors do not define new `SO(3)` irreps beyond those pieces; antisymmetric index pairs are dualized by `\epsilon_{ijk}` into lower-rank ordinary or pseudo tensors.

## Domain Consequences

- Status: Proven. A sector carrying an odd number of epsilon dualizations is a pseudo tensor or pseudo scalar.
- Status: Proven. By assumption `A2`, parity-odd or pseudo sectors are outside the current theorem domain.
- Status: Proven. A sector carrying an even number of epsilon dualizations returns to an ordinary tensor sector.
- Status: Proven. Ordinary tensor sectors decompose further into STF tensors plus trace descendants by the same imported `O(3)` decomposition.

## Delimitation

- Status: Proven. Mixed symmetry is therefore not excluded automatically at the `GL(3)` level.
- Status: Proven. It is excluded or absorbed only after the explicit `O(3)` irrep decomposition is applied.
- Status: Note. This is exactly why the present theorem is stated as an irreducible-envelope theorem rather than as a raw index-symmetry slogan.

## Verdict

- Status: Proven. Inside the parity-even nonspinning local theorem domain, every genuinely new primitive tensor family must reduce to an ordinary STF sector once pseudo pieces and traces are removed.
