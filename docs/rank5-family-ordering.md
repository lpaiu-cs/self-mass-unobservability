# Rank-5 Family Ordering

## Primitive-Family Definition

- Status: Proven. `Rodd5+` denotes a genuinely new local parity-even rank-5 primitive family `U_{ijklm}` in the MVP free-fall sector.
- Status: Proven. Trace descendants of a rank-5 tensor are not counted as new primitive-family admissions here, because those traces reduce to already audited lower-rank scalar, vector, rank-3, or rank-4 family classes.
- Status: Proven. Derivative-generated rank-5 blocks such as `\nabla Q`, `\nabla T`, higher-gradient vector descendants, or scalar descendants dressed only by derivatives are assigned to their parent audited families and are not double-counted as a new primitive-family admission.

## Representative Choice

- Status: Proven. The audit uses a fully symmetric trace-free rank-5 representative `U_{ijklm}`.
- Status: Proven. This is the minimally irreducible parity-even representative under the current MVP assumptions: traces collapse to already audited lower-rank classes, while antisymmetric or spin-carrying pieces are already outside the theorem domain by assumption `A2`.
- Status: Proven. No special rank-5 background restriction, absorption rule, or ordering assumption is being used tacitly in this audit.

## Physical Question

- Status: Proven. The physical question is whether unsuppressed admission of a genuine primitive rank-5 family produces a new `\Delta \le 4` survivor beyond the enlarged audited-set baseline `{R2, R0a, R0b, R1, Rodd+, Reven4+}`.
- Status: Proven. If such a survivor appears, it obstructs promotion of the current enlarged audited-set result to MVP-envelope sufficiency.
