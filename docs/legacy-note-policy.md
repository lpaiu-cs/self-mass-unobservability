# Legacy Note Policy

- Status: Proven. This policy defines how theorem-facing notes are classified in the closed theorem package.

## Authoritative

- Status: Proven. Authoritative files are the paper-facing front door of the repo.
- Status: Proven. They may state theorem-domain closure, the negative uniqueness no-go, and the sharp boundary escape map.
- Status: Proven. They must not read like milestone diaries or open bottleneck trackers.

## Supporting

- Status: Proven. Supporting files provide the technical theorem notes, lemma-level scaffolding, or exact assumption ledgers behind the authoritative package.
- Status: Proven. They may be detailed, but they should not compete with the front-door theorem statement.

## Historical

- Status: Proven. Historical files preserve earlier theorem attempts, failed stronger theorems, provisional bridge notes, or milestone chronology.
- Status: Proven. Historical files must carry an explicit banner so that they are not mistaken for the current theorem front door.

## Raw Bookkeeping

- Status: Proven. Raw-bookkeeping files preserve survivor counts, rank and nullity ledgers, exhaustive audit tables, or compression tables.
- Status: Proven. Raw bookkeeping is not a theorem statement unless an authoritative or supporting theorem note explicitly promotes it.
- Status: Proven. In particular, raw survivor-count, rank, and nullity notes do not by themselves constitute a corrected basis theorem.
