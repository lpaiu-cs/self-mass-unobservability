# Nonanalytic activation boundary

Status: Proven. The smooth-flat response `m=m0+alpha phi(Y)` with phi=exp(-1/Y^2) for Y>0 and zero otherwise is local and C-infinity. Every derivative of phi at zero vanishes. Its finite-order jet is valid with a beyond-all-orders remainder.

Status: Proven. For nonzero alpha, its exact germ differs from its constant Taylor series. This violates analyticity A5 but does not refute the finite-order expansion. The previous claim of a finite-jet escape is withdrawn.

Status: Proven. The square-root activation at a threshold is a separate example lacking the regularity required by an ordinary finite-order Taylor approximation. It does not enlarge the declared polynomial catalog.
